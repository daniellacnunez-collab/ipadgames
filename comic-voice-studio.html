HOW 3+ STUDENTS WORK SEPARATELY

  1. 1
    Set Up Your Cast (3, 4, 5+ Characters): Customize each character's name, student name, and color badge in the Cast Roster. Need more roles? Click "+ Add Character / Student"!
  2. 2
    Select Your Role & Record: In "Who is recording right now?", click your name. Only your speech bubbles unlock (Your Line!) while teammates' bubbles stay safely locked.
  3. 3
    Export Your Audio Pack (.json): When finished, click "Export Audio (.json)" on your character card and share that small file with your group or teacher.
  4. 4
    Merge Every Teammate's Clips: Click "Merge Teammate Audio (.json)" one after another to load Student A, Student B, Student C, and beyond into one master comic—then press "Play Full Comic"!
